### Ruthless Synthesis: Why `branch_1` Survived and `branch_2` Failed

#### 1. Disqualification of `branch_2`: Structural Inefficiency & Memory Footprint
* **Flawed Topology:** `branch_2` implements an event sweep that materializes ticket interval collections in memory before running a secondary pass to accumulate business minutes. 
* **Allocator & Cache Pressure:** Materializing discrete interval representations per ticket incurs intermediate heap allocations ($O(K)$ interval objects per ticket) and cache misses. Under high event churn, this guarantees garbage collection pressure and cache line thrashing.
* **Redundant Complexity:** Deferring the calculation into a two-phase sweep (reconstruct intervals $\to$ iterate and count) doubles the traversal overhead when the state can be resolved sequentially.

#### 2. The Superiority of `branch_1`: Deterministic Closed-Form State Machine
* **$O(1)$ Space, In-Flight Accumulation:** `branch_1` avoids intermediate allocations entirely. It models ticket lifecycle transitions inline as a per-ticket state machine, maintaining only the running state and the previous transition timestamp.
* **Closed-Form Arithmetic:** Instead of enumerating and bucketing raw intervals, `branch_1` evaluates business minutes algebraically across schedule boundaries. This converts an iterative scan into constant-time arithmetic per lifecycle transition.
* **Deterministic Throughput:** Because work is strictly bounded to state updates at the boundary of incoming events, `branch_1` guarantees zero buffer bloat, zero allocation churn, and predictable execution latency.
